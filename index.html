Explore · Serve · Reflect

Leadership starts beyond the classroom.

LeadershipTreks is a student-led organization founded by sophomores at Livingston High School. We take students into real workplaces, community projects, and guided reflection so they can see leadership in action and practice it themselves.

Our mission

To build capable young leaders and inspire them to lead outside the classroom.

Leadership is becoming more important in the era of AI, but most students have few chances to practice it where it counts. A Trek is a structured outing with a clear purpose: learn from people who are already making an impact, give back to a community, and make sense of what you saw.

Why we exist

Students need more than a classroom to learn leadership.

  1. Students struggle to take initiative outside the classroom.

    Inside Higher Ed's Student Voice survey found that “over a third of [college] students (36 percent) haven’t participated in a single extra- or co-curricular activity.”

  2. Real-world exposure is limited.

    Few students ever see what a workplace or a professional career actually looks like.

  3. Students lack practice reflecting and growing.

    Experience only turns into leadership when someone stops to learn from it.

How we see it
Explore. Serve. Reflect.

Go see something new, contribute to something larger than yourself, then think about what it taught you. Each Trek is built around that loop.

Programs

Three ways to practice leadership.

CareerTrek and ServiceTrek are both in planning. Together with ReflectionTrek, they make up one framework for growing as a leader.

First program in planning

CareerTrek

Explore leadership in the real world.

CareerTrek gives students the opportunity to step into professional environments, connect with people in different fields, and discover how leadership works beyond the classroom. Through workplace visits, conversations with professionals, and career exploration, students gain exposure to new perspectives and possibilities for their futures.

A CareerTrek is hosted by an organization at its workplace and is planned for about two hours with 20–30 students.

Focus
Career exploration, networking, and leadership in action.
What students do
Visit workplaces, meet professionals, ask questions, and learn about different career paths.
What students gain
A better understanding of the working world, new connections, and insight into their own interests and goals.
Planned agenda
CareerTrek agenda
TimeSegment
10:00–10:15Welcome. Host introduces themselves; a LeadershipTreks executive explains the day.
10:15–10:45Company overview. What the organization does, key roles, career paths, and a Q&A.
10:45–11:50Panel & conversation. Employees share their experience, moderated by a LeadershipTreks executive, with open student Q&A.
11:50–12:00Closing. Thank-yous, student reflection, wrap-up.

Times are a sample schedule. The final agenda is set with each host.

In planning

ServiceTrek

Turn leadership into action.

ServiceTrek gives students opportunities to address real community needs through meaningful service. By working with community organizations and collaborating with others, students learn that leadership is not just about directing people. It is also about recognizing problems, taking initiative, and making a difference.

Focus
Community impact, initiative, and collaboration.
What students do
Participate in service projects, work with local organizations, and contribute to community initiatives.
What students gain
A stronger sense of responsibility, experience working toward a shared goal, and an understanding of how leadership can serve others.

ReflectionTrek

Understand yourself. Grow as a leader.

ReflectionTrek gives students the space to examine their experiences, understand their strengths, and identify areas where they can grow. Through guided discussions, individual reflection, and collaborative activities, students learn to turn experiences into lessons they can apply to future challenges.

Focus
Self-awareness, personal growth, and continuous improvement.
What students do
Reflect on experiences, discuss challenges, explore their values, and set personal leadership goals.
What students gain
A clearer understanding of themselves, greater confidence in their decisions, and a plan for continued growth.
